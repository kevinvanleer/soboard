# So Board! Privacy Policy

So Board! keeps an image, such as a boarding pass or a recipe, on screen and over the lock screen. It is published by Kevin Van Leer.

## Your images

Images you add are copied into the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded, and the author has no access to them. Deleting an item in the app, or uninstalling the app, deletes its copy.

Boarding-pass barcodes and text are read on your device to suggest a name for the image. Nothing read from an image leaves your device.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner ad using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google collects and shares:

- the device's advertising ID
- approximate location derived from your IP address
- ad interactions and diagnostic data

This data goes to Google, not to the author. Google describes how it uses it at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings under Privacy, Ads.

## What the author collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
